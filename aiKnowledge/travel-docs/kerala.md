Category: Destination
Country: India

# Overview

Kerala is one of India's most scenic travel destinations, located along the southwestern coast of the country. It is internationally recognized for its backwaters, hill stations, tea plantations, wildlife sanctuaries, Ayurveda, beaches, and rich cultural heritage.

Kerala is often referred to as **"God's Own Country"** because of its natural beauty and diverse landscapes.

The destination attracts honeymoon couples, families, solo travelers, nature enthusiasts, photographers, wellness tourists, and international visitors.

---

# Why Visit Kerala

Kerala offers a combination of:

- Backwater cruises
- Mountain landscapes
- Beaches
- Wildlife
- Ayurvedic wellness
- Cultural festivals
- Traditional cuisine
- Tea and spice plantations

Unlike many destinations, travelers can experience mountains, forests, lakes, rivers, and beaches within a single trip.

# Best Time to Visit

## October to March

The most pleasant season.

Ideal for:

- Sightseeing
- Houseboats
- Beaches
- Wildlife

Temperature generally ranges between 20°C and 32°C.

---

## April to May

Warmer weather.

Suitable for hill stations like Munnar.

---

## June to September

Monsoon season.

Recommended for:

- Ayurveda treatments
- Nature photography
- Waterfalls
- Green landscapes

Heavy rainfall may affect outdoor activities.
